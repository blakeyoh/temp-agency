# Sweet 16 and Elite 8 Protocol

> **Status:** Approved in principle by the commissioner on 2026-08-27. Preparation is in
> progress. No scrimmage or Sweet 16 game has begun. `commissioner-rulings.md` remains the
> source of truth if this document and a later ruling disagree.

## Governing objective

The tournament exists to find generative processes that move a model past the competent,
legible idea around #12 and toward the strange, causally useful idea around #99. The Round
of 32 established that comparative judging, fixed reference sets, isolated panels and visible
commissioner intervention produce better evidence than solo absolute scoring. It did not
observe any entrant generating an actual solution.

The next two rounds therefore add live evidence while preserving the existing five-axis
rubric. Output yield is a separate signal rather than a sixth weighted axis.

## What the Round of 32 could not establish

1. **The packets contained mechanism descriptions, not mechanism-produced outputs.** Panel
   predicates often supplied hypothetical outputs themselves. Those examples were useful
   reasoning, but they were not observations.
2. **Twelve of sixteen survivors require a missing runtime surface.** Some can be enacted
   manually; others remain future-state hypotheses. The difference must be visible.
3. **Compounding was projected from the mechanism description.** No entrant demonstrated
   what its tenth use produces.
4. **The evaluator population was fixed.** Reusing the same three panels indefinitely risks
   selecting for the stable preferences of Builder, Skeptic and Ecologist.
5. **Selection has outpaced generation.** Several genuinely new mechanisms appeared inside
   panel commentary, but the process had no artifact for keeping them.

## Required order before any scrimmage

The order is binding unless the commissioner changes it explicitly.

1. Record the approved next-round procedure and commissioner directives.
2. Create `collision-residue.md` and seed it with the three Round-of-32 discoveries.
3. Create the missing `parallel-track.md` and reconstruct the durable R32 separations.
4. Draft and freeze an evidence contract for every Sweet 16 entrant.
5. Resolve each of the five carried defects by explicit commissioner amendment or a visible
   unresolved flag. No quiet repair is allowed. **Completed by Ruling 11:** all five enter
   their scrimmages flagged.
6. Complete issue #20: add unresolved internal tensions to the roster and require a
   Sacrifice Receipt in future verdicts.
7. Freeze the roster and knowledge-pack commit used by the round.
8. Run one unscored scrimmage per entrant.
9. Present any scrimmage-derived amendment to the commissioner, then freeze the official
   entrant packets.
10. Begin the Sweet 16 Tail Test.

## Issue #21 decision: deferred as a round blocker

Full knowledge-pack expansion is deferred unless the commissioner later revives it as a
prerequisite. Excluding every incomplete profile from judging would remove eight of the
roster's most differentiating perspectives: Anthropologist, Chief of Staff, Civil Rights
Activist, Contrarian, Executive Coach, Left-Fielder, Missiologist and Pediatric Occupational
Therapist. That removes the default lens, the explicit random-domain importer, and several
of the roster's strongest power, culture, agency and development frames at the moment when
evaluator monoculture is itself a tournament risk.

For the Sweet 16 and Elite 8:

- all 24 built profiles remain eligible to judge;
- every fresh panel must contain at least one specialist with a conforming
  `knowledge/<slug>/positions.md`;
- the draw map records whether each panel member had a complete pack;
- a panel with two incomplete-pack specialists is invalid and must be redrawn from the same
  seed using the next eligible pairing;
- the round records the exact roster and knowledge-pack commit before dispatch.

Issue #21 remains a direct prerequisite for the Voice Oracle's training claim and a corpus
defect for the Oblique Deck. Their evidence contracts carry those limitations visibly.

## The unscored scrimmage

The scrimmage is a protected enactment, not a preliminary score. Each entrant receives a
small neutral brief and produces:

1. the mechanism output;
2. an execution trace naming what ran;
3. a simulation disclosure naming every step performed manually or by model judgment;
4. the predicted signature from its evidence contract;
5. the exact point at which the mechanism became ambiguous, if any.

The scrimmage cannot eliminate an entrant. It can expose an undefined operation, false
claim, silent fallback or implementation gap. One amendment may be proposed per entrant.
Every amendment requires commissioner approval and must satisfy the standing amendment rule:
it is specific, serves the entrant's existing thesis and is recorded with its source.
Every run uses `scrimmage-template.md`; an informal transcript is not a completed scrimmage.

After rulings, the definitions and execution substitutions are frozen for the official run.
Readiness and freeze state are tracked in `pre-s16-readiness.md`.

## Sweet 16: the Tail Test

### Live generation

All sixteen entrants receive the same unseen divergent brief after their contracts are
frozen. Each produces 24 numbered proposals under a matched base model, context allowance
and token budget. The generation packet requests all 24 sincerely and does not disclose that
ideas 17-24 are the primary comparison window.

The official artifact contains all 24 ideas so panels can check repetition and novelty decay.
The late window is highlighted only in the judging packet. Each late idea must retain a
causal return path to the original problem; distance without a return path is noise.

Where an entrant cannot be faithfully enacted, it submits no fabricated demonstration. It
receives a visible `PROMISE` flag and a falsifiable build experiment. A `PROMISE` game is
automatically CONTESTED when the available output evidence and the mechanism verdict point
in different directions.

### Two-pass judging

Each panel completes two sealed passes in order.

**Pass 1: output-only yield vote**

The panel sees anonymized A/B outputs without mechanism names, seeds, regions, authorship,
contracts or prior results. It must:

1. name the strongest surprising proposal from A and from B;
2. state the causal return path from each selected proposal to the brief;
3. identify where each side begins repeating its early moves;
4. vote A, B or Tie on late-window creative yield.

Three panel votes produce the **yield winner**. The yield vote is reported separately from
the five-axis total.

**Pass 2: mechanism-and-trace verdict**

The same panel receives the frozen definitions, evidence contracts and execution traces. It
scores the existing five axes against `reference-sets.md`, supplies every required predicate,
names an overall winner and applies the three-test absorption standard.

The game is CONTESTED when any of these disagree:

- yield winner;
- five-axis aggregate;
- panel majority on the overall winner;
- faithful-enactment status.

The commissioner resolves the disagreement. No precedence is implied.

### Sacrifice Receipt

Every panel verdict must include:

> **SACRIFICE RECEIPT:** I honored `[principle or bias]`, sacrificed `[competing principle]`,
> and accepted `[specific cost or risk]`.

The receipt is valid only when the two named commitments genuinely pull in different
directions in this game. “Both were satisfied” fails the requirement. The receipt operationalizes
issue #20 and makes bias-defying votes visible instead of accidental.

### Collision Residue

After the panel seals its vote and absorption disposition, it answers:

> What mechanism becomes visible only because these two entrants collided and is contained
> in neither entrant?

The candidate must differ from both parents on at least one declared axis among mechanism,
actor, failure mode or timescale. It cannot alter the current result or enter the current
field. It is recorded in `collision-residue.md` as a candidate for a future field.

### Sweet 16 panels

- **Calibration anchor:** Builder, because faithful enactment is the round's immediate
  vulnerability.
- **Fresh panels:** two high-contrast lead/lens pairs drawn after outputs and packets are
  sealed.
- **Eligibility:** the issue #21 guardrail above applies.
- **Isolation:** panels see neither one another's work nor prior-round verdicts.

The round draw map must include exactly three `panels` records. Each record names a stable
display `name`, lowercase filename-safe `file_tag`, lead and lens slugs, and each member's
pack-completeness status. `tally.py` reads those records rather than assuming the R32 panel
ecology. It must also set `"require_live_evidence": true`; the tally then refuses an
incomplete yield vote or faithful-enactment record and includes both signals in the result.
R32 remains backward-compatible through its legacy three-panel default.

```json
{
  "require_live_evidence": true,
  "panels": [
    {
      "name": "Builder",
      "file_tag": "builder",
      "lead": "nuclear-reactor-operator",
      "lens": "magician-illusionist",
      "lead_pack": "complete",
      "lens_pack": "complete",
      "fresh": false
    }
  ]
}
```

The complete array has one calibration anchor and two records with `"fresh": true`. The
tally rejects a live-evidence draw with any missing pack status or a fresh panel whose two
members are both marked `incomplete`.

The Sweet 16 stops after all eight games are tallied. Results, disagreements, absorptions,
Sacrifice Receipts and Collision Residues are presented to the commissioner before any Elite
8 packet is created.

## Elite 8: the Return Test

The Elite 8 receives a new brief from a different task class. The round tests whether a
mechanism can bring strangeness back into a coherent, causally useful recommendation rather
than merely sustain a long list.

Each matchup produces three artifacts:

1. A's solo output;
2. B's solo output;
3. one directional contact run, A→B or B→A, selected by a recorded seed after both solo
   outputs are sealed.

The contact run does not determine the head-to-head winner. It reveals whether the mechanisms
amplify, dampen, exploit or ignore one another. Useful pairings are recorded in
`parallel-track.md` as coalitions without changing either entrant's definition.

Elite 8 judging retains the two-pass structure. The Ecologist becomes the calibration anchor
because cross-context durability and compounding are now observable. Two new high-contrast
panels are drawn under the same issue #21 guardrail.

The tournament stops again after the Elite 8. The commissioner receives the Final Four,
the updated parallel architecture, the Collision Residue ledger and every contested game
before the Final Four format is designed.

## Two final products

The tournament is designed to yield two legitimate winners:

1. **The bracket champion:** the strongest individual mechanism under the tournament ruler.
2. **The strange-making architecture:** the smallest useful set of orthogonal mechanisms and
   coalitions spanning input, persona, generation, memory and evaluation.

The champion answers “which single mechanism survived?” The architecture answers “what should
this repo actually become?”
