### Explicit carried-defect test

The noun mask did not remove all priors. Three untouched words leaked structure into the masked reasoning:

1. `open` is a verb that carries the prior that ENTITY_A is an interaction with a beginning, and that the useful response should be an initiating move or opener. That prior survives even when `conversation` is hidden.
2. `List` is a verb that carries the prior that the response should be ten separately enumerated candidates rather than one flowing exchange, a script, or a discussion of criteria. It shapes the proposal before any noun is restored.
3. `distinct` is an adjective that carries the prior that the ten candidates must be nonredundant. It pushed the masked proposal toward ten different interaction structures, such as reason, preference, observation, comparison, and exchange, instead of ten phrasings of one move.

`open` is the strongest domain leak. `List` and `distinct` preserve output and task priors rather than specific conference knowledge, but they still disprove the broader claim that removing the nouns removes the priors. The useful mechanism is narrower than that claim.
