# The Field of 32

> **Premise.** In a creative workshop, groups are asked for 100 ways to protect a home.
> The first ten are a fence, a dog, a camera. The last ten are a moat and a UFO. The
> value is not in the moat — it is in the fact that reaching idea #99 *requires* a
> different generative process than reaching idea #3.
>
> temp-agency v2 reliably reaches idea #12: a competent lead, a competent lens, a
> disagreement that is genuinely useful and entirely legible. The evidence gate proved
> the lens changes decisions. It did not prove the lens produces anything *strange*.
> This field is 32 mechanisms for pushing the repo from #12 to #99.

**Provenance:** entries marked ◆ are the owner's, expanded where flagged as vague enough
to split. Entries marked ◇ are generated to fill the field to 32.

**Distinctness rule:** no two entries may share a primary mechanism. Several entries
attack the same target (the model's prior) but must do so by different means —
prohibition, entropy, execution, and memory are four different means, and the field is
organized around them.

### What the surviving field collectively requires

which games are really contests between *versions of the same build*.

### How to read each entry below

Every entrant carries the same trailer. Absent fields are marked `—` rather than omitted, so a
blank is visibly a blank rather than an oversight.

- **Status** — outcome, game, aggregate score, and how it was decided.
- **Enhancements** — what the entrant *gained* by absorbing another, including what was
  explicitly refused and any dissent preserved on the record. Blank unless it absorbed or was
  amended.
- **What travelled** — for eliminated entrants whose mechanism was taken: what went, and where.
- **Gaps** — what this repo would need, in three slots: *Build* (new artifacts), *Wire* (where
  it attaches to what exists), *Absent* (what is missing today, stated as fact).
- **Carried defect** — a verified authoring or design defect, where one exists.
- **Note** / **Held because** / **Flagged for separate build** — context the panels produced
  that would otherwise only survive in the raw verdicts.

## ENTROPY REGION
*Mechanisms that inject genuine randomness or genuine outside material into the process.
The shared enemy: a model asked to "be random" retrieves the most-likely random-seeming
answer from its own prior.*

### E1 · The Entropy Well ◆
*(owner idea 2, split: "scripts and shells")*

Randomness in this repo is currently rhetorical. Make it literal, seeded, and auditable.
`bin/draw` is a real PRNG that emits a seed; every stochastic choice in a dispatch —
which lead, which lens, which constraint card, which mutation — derives from that one
seed. The seed is stamped in the output header. Same seed, same world; no seed, a
different world. Creativity becomes reproducible, and therefore debuggable.

**Not native:** models simulate randomness by reaching for the most-likely
random-*sounding* option. A real PRNG has no favorite number.

**Status** · **ADVANCED → Sweet 16** — Dog G6, 18–13, panels 2–1, no ruling required. All
absorptions refused (Skeptic SUBSUMED, two ORTHOGONAL).

**Enhancements** · Not an absorption — an **amendment** [Ruling 14 / Amendment 4]. E1's
scrimmage showed the PRNG genuinely drew a seed, but nothing confirmed the prose actually
tracked the drawn indices — the mapping from seed to output was unchecked
(`s16-e1.md:175,182`). **Amended definition:** an official run must include one counterfactual
replay — re-draw at least one index under a second stamped seed and confirm the prose for that
item changes materially. A replay that leaves the prose unchanged records the run as decorative
randomness, a contract failure rather than a pass. Load-bearing beyond E1 itself: E6's card draw
and A6's mutation seed both assume a seed actually moves the output.

**Gaps** · *Build:* `bin/draw`, a real PRNG that emits a seed and derives every stochastic
choice from it; a seed-stamped dispatch header. · *Wire:* `SKILL.md` §Staffing Logic steps
1–2 — lead and lens are chosen by contextual match today, so there is no stochastic choice
to seed until one is introduced. · *Absent:* no `bin/` directory; no record of any past
dispatch, so "same seed, same world" has nothing to replay against.

**Note** · `docs/plan-v2.md` hypothesis 3 explicitly **rejected** a random mode ("no random
mode — left-fielder profile already covers serendipity"). This entrant reopens a settled
decision, which is worth naming before it is built rather than after. Separately, the
Builder — the panel with a disclosed bias *toward* Mechanism — voted against the mechanism
here: *"A PRNG constrains a distribution, not an output, and the ruler asks for an output."*
A vote that defies its own declared bias carries more weight, not less.

### E2 · Cross-Repo Foraging ◇

Import a real object from outside, not a remembered one. Before the persona speaks, the
harness fetches one concrete external artifact — a random file from an unrelated local
repo, a random paragraph from the persona's own `knowledge/canon.md`, a random line from
a book, a random Wikipedia page. The persona must make it **load-bearing**: if the
foraged object appears only in the opening paragraph and does no work thereafter, the
pass is rejected.

**Not native:** a model asked to bring in something unexpected retrieves from its own
priors. Genuinely foraged text is outside the prior by construction.

**Status** · **ADVANCED → Sweet 16** — Fence G3, **16–16 dead heat**, commissioner tiebreak
[Ruling 2]. Panels leaned 2–1 to Foraging. Took Distance +7 and Irreducibility +9 unanimous
and maximal; lost Compounding +9 unanimous and maximal.

**Enhancements** · Not an absorption — an **amendment** [Ruling 15 / Amendment 5], shared with
`E3`. E2's scrimmage fetched a real artifact, but the "load-bearing" check was operator
self-review with no authority to reject — 7 of 10 items were untouched by the foraged object
(`s16-e2.md:236,249`). **Amended definition:** the load-bearing check becomes a per-item
deletion test with real teeth — if removing the foraged artifact leaves an item's substance
unchanged, that item is rejected and regenerated under the artifact. A pass requires at least
three surviving artifact-dependent items, replacing operator judgment with a mechanical gate.

**Gaps** · *Build:* a forager with named, reachable sources; a **load-bearing check** with
the authority to reject a pass when the foraged object does no work past the opening
paragraph. · *Wire:* a pre-pass step before the persona reads the task (`SKILL.md` step 3–4)
— the same missing surface `A1` and `C8` need. · *Absent:* no fetch path is defined and
network access at dispatch time is unspecified; **8 of 24 specialists have no conforming
knowledge pack**, so "a random paragraph from the persona's own `canon.md`" is unavailable
for a third of the roster.

**Note** · The most irreducible entrant in the field — a base model reproduces 5–8% of it —
and entirely memoryless. Its tie with the Novelty Gate is the tournament's sharpest
statement of **irreducibility versus compounding**, and that trade-off is unresolved.

### E3 · The Wrong Expert on Purpose ◇

The weirdness currently lives in the LENS, and the LENS is the junior role. Invert it.
Staff the *least* relevant specialist as LEAD and let the domain-appropriate expert be
the LENS. The soccer referee leads the database migration; the systems thinker gets to
object. The wrong expert has to derive the whole answer from their own primitives, and
that derivation is where non-obvious structure comes from.

**Not native:** the model's strongest reflex is domain-appropriate matching. This is a
deliberate defeat of that reflex, at the position of maximum authority.

**Status** · **ADVANCED → Sweet 16** — Fence G1, lost the aggregate 8–12, won the panel
majority 2–1, **commissioner overrule** [Ruling 1]. All three panels returned ORTHOGONAL
against The Understudy; the merge was refused three times independently.

**Enhancements** · Not an absorption — an **amendment** [Ruling 15 / Amendment 5], shared with
`E2`. E3's scrimmage ran the inversion, but the wrong expert's derivation only restructured the
answer's spine — the ten surface items stayed conventional under control-room labels, a "thin
costume" the record names against itself (`s16-e3.md:149-151,174`). **Amended definition:** the
wrong-expert frame is subject to the same per-item deletion test as `E2` — if removing the
wrong-expert derivation leaves an item's substance unchanged, the item is rejected and
regenerated under the frame, with at least three items required to survive it. Forces the
mismatch to actually deform the output rather than relabel it.

**Gaps** · *Build:* a "least relevant" selector over `references/roster.md` — the inverse of
the contextual match the repo performs today; a rule that stops the demoted domain expert's
objection from silently becoming the answer. · *Wire:* `SKILL.md` §Lead selection and §Lens
selection, both of which currently optimize for fit ("match the primary domain of the task
to the specialist whose way of seeing fits best"). · *Absent:* no distance metric between a
task and a specialist, and nothing records which pairings have already been tried.

**Note** · The v1 run scored this **dead last in the field of 32** (25/50) by solo absolute
scoring; three blind panels then ranked it top of its bracket on Distance. It is the
clearest single piece of evidence that the v1 method was wrong.

### E4 · Roster Mutation ◇

Personas breed. `bin/breed <a> <b> --seed N` performs structural crossover on two
profiles — Principles from A, Methodology from B, Anti-Patterns interleaved — then
applies point mutations: reverse a principle's polarity, delete a methodology phase,
import a foreign Voice register. The child is a one-off used for a single task, scored,
then promoted to `roster/f1/` or killed. The roster evolves instead of being authored.

**Not native:** asked to combine two personas, a model averages toward the generic.
Structural crossover with mutation produces combinations no author would choose.

**Status** · **ADVANCED → Sweet 16 as THE BREEDING PROGRAM** — Moat G11, 17–14, all three
panels named it the winner, **absorption ratified 2–1** [Ruling 7]. Won Mechanism +9
unanimous.

**Enhancements** · Absorbs Failure Archaeology's **killed hybrids with a recorded cause of
death** — narrowly scoped, explicitly *not* its full claim over every rejected idea in the
repo. The growth is from memoryless random search into actual selection: *"a breeding
program with no record of what died is not performing selection; it is performing
memoryless random search over a finite seed space it will re-draw forever"* (Ecologist).
The kill step already exists in the entrant as written — *"scored, then promoted to
`roster/f1/` or killed"* — and its corpses currently vanish. · **Dissent preserved
(Skeptic):** in a project that has disclaimed efficiency, re-rolling a dead combination is
*waste*, not harm — *"B gets slower, not worse."*

**Gaps** · *Build:* `bin/breed <a> <b> --seed N` performing structural crossover over
profile sections plus point mutations; `roster/f1/` for promoted children; the cause-of-death
record for killed ones; a scoring step that decides promote-or-kill. · *Wire:*
`roster/TEMPLATE.md` is the crossover contract — structural crossover assumes the 24
profiles are section-uniform, which has never been checked. · *Absent:* no `bin/`, no
`roster/f1/`, no kill record, and no per-dispatch score for selection to act on.

### E5 · Temporal Displacement ◇

Every persona is silently contemporary. Date them. A `--era` flag instantiates the
specialist at a specific year with **only that year's primitives**. A 1911 systems
thinker has no feedback-loop vocabulary and must reach for hydraulics, railway
signalling, and stockyards. A 2140 one has vocabulary we have not invented and must
invent it. The anachronism forbids retrieval and forces re-derivation.

**Not native:** a model will happily write in a period *style* while keeping every modern
concept. This forbids the concept, not the diction — a much harder constraint.

**Status** · **ADVANCED → Sweet 16 as THE DATED SPECIALIST** — Dog G5, 17–13, panels 2–1,
**absorption accepted, modified** [Ruling 4]. Absorption split 1–2 and was accepted anyway
on the commissioner's condition that it harden the work.

**Enhancements** · Absorbs the Constraint Compiler's **era-lexicon checker only**, modified
so that it **logs before it rejects**. Builder and Ecologist ran the same deletion test and
reached opposite conclusions — one said removing the checker degrades the idea to period
diction carrying modern concepts, the other said *"the leaked modern term is A's
highest-scoring feature; linting it away destroys the diagnostic."* Both hold if the leak is
captured as evidence first and the output regenerated second. The compile-from-Anti-Patterns
half is **explicitly refused**: it carries the Compiler's rival diagnosis that voice prose is
the homogeneity source. · **Merged definition:** *"Instantiate the specialist at a named year
and reject any output containing a term that postdates it, recording each rejection as
evidence of which modern concept the answer could not be built without."*

**Gaps** · *Build:* a dated term lexicon per era band; a post-generation checker; a leak log
that survives the regeneration. · *Wire:* an `--era` flag on `/temp-agency-plan` and
`/temp-agency-review`; profiles would need a plausible date range each — a nuclear reactor
operator instantiated at 1911 is incoherent, and nothing in `roster/*.md` carries a date.
· *Absent:* no per-profile dating, and **no post-generation check of any kind runs in this
repo today** — the checker is the first of five Sweet 16 entrants to need that surface.

### E6 · The Oblique Deck ◇

Brian Eno's *Oblique Strategies*, compiled from this repo's own roster and obeyed
**literally**. Every `knowledge/*/positions.md` is distilled into single-line imperatives
— "Ask what the soil requires." "Name who pays and isn't in the room." "Restart from the
last honest state." One card is drawn per pass, and the persona must obey it literally
*even when it is a bad fit*. The friction of a card that doesn't belong is the generator.

**Not native:** a model given a suggestion applies it where it fits and quietly drops it
where it doesn't. Literal obedience to a bad-fit instruction is not a default behavior.

**Status** · **ADVANCED → Sweet 16** — UFO G16, 15–10, panels 2–1, CONTESTED on the split
[Ruling 10]. All absorptions refused.

**Enhancements** · Not an absorption — an **amendment** [Ruling 19 / Amendment 7]. E6's
scrimmage drew genuinely, but only over a deck the operator had hand-built from 2 of 16
available `positions.md` files — the randomness only ever chose among options already curated
(`s16-e6.md:33-37,101`). **Amended definition:** an official run counts the real
`knowledge/*/positions.md` files at dispatch, and the seeded draw selects both the source packs
and the card from that full counted corpus; the operator may not pre-select which packs feed
distillation, and a missing requested pack fails the draw closed. Pushes randomness upstream of
curation, widening the tail the draw can actually reach — complements, but does not resolve, the
carried corpus defect below (only 16 of 24 packs exist at all).

**Gaps** · *Build:* a distiller turning `knowledge/*/positions.md` into single-line
imperatives; the deck file itself; a draw step; a check that the card was obeyed **literally**
rather than applied where convenient — literal obedience to a bad-fit card is the entire
mechanism and nothing currently verifies it. · *Wire:* `SKILL.md` step 4, plus the same
seeded-draw surface `E1` needs. · *Absent:* 8 of 24 specialists have no conforming pack, so
one-third of the deck needs another source **by construction**, not by oversight.

**Carried defect** · The corpus claim is overstated. Only **16 of 24** specialists have a
`positions.md` (~160 bullet lines total); of the entrant's three sample cards, one fairly
distils real text, one appears in no `positions.md`, and one appears **nowhere in the repo**.
The Contrarian — the default lens designated by `SKILL.md` — has no pack at all. Tracked in
issue #21.

### E7 · Stochastic Persona Fracture ◇

Real experts contain contradictions; a persona file resolves them into a coherent voice.
Mid-pass, the persona is forcibly bisected along a fault line named in its own profile —
the farmer's patience against the farmer's seasonal deadline, the monk's simplicity
against the monk's obligation to the poor. Two sub-personas argue, and the **unresolved**
argument is part of the output. One roster entry, two disagreeing voices.

**Not native:** models resolve internal tension into a single coherent register. This
forbids the resolution and ships the tension.

**Status** · **ELIMINATED** — Moat G9, 2–37. The widest margin of the tournament; the winner
took four of five axes at +9 unanimous and maximal, and this entrant scored on Compounding
alone. All absorptions refused.

**Enhancements** · —

**Gaps** · *Build:* the fault lines themselves — a required `## Internal Tensions` section in
`roster/TEMPLATE.md`, backfilled across 24 profiles. **Implemented on the tournament branch
before the Sweet 16.** · *Wire:* `SKILL.md` step 4 (the pass), where the bisection would
happen; that runtime remains absent.

**Historical authoring defect, since repaired in the repo** · The entrant cited a fault line
that did not exist during R32. The Skeptic checked
`roster/franciscan-monk.md` and found no "simplicity against obligation to the poor" tension
named anywhere in it. Verified across the whole roster: **0 of 24 built profiles name any
internal tension, conflict, or trade-off within their Core Principles.** The panel escalated
this from a finding about the entrant to a finding about the **repo** — filed as issue #20,
and plausibly a direct cause of the homogeneity this tournament exists to fight. Before the
Sweet 16, the template and all 24 profiles were backfilled with unresolved internal tensions;
the original entrant remains eliminated and its R32 result is unchanged.

**Flagged for separate build** · *Disagreement sourced from differing evidence* (Ecologist,
G9): two transforms, two halves, two documents — *"a different and better object than
disagreement sourced from instructed tension, and only the first is un-simulatable."*

### E8 · Temperature Choreography ◆
*(owner idea 4, split: "workflows to force creativity")*

The sampling schedule is currently invisible and flat. Choreograph it into three
movements. **HOT:** many short high-variance samples, quantity mandated, no quality
filter permitted. **COLD:** a separate agent that may only prune, never generate.
**AUDIT:** a third agent that verifies at least one survivor came from the top decile of
weirdness in the HOT pass — if the weirdest survivor is missing, the whole pass reruns.

**Not native:** a single pass silently converges and no one can see what it discarded.
Separating generation from judgment makes the discard auditable.

**Status** · **ELIMINATED** — UFO G15, 16–19, unanimous on the winner and the closest
unanimous game of the round. All absorptions refused.

**Enhancements** · —

**Gaps** · *Build:* a weirdness measure with a named calibration — the decisive objection was
that *"top decile of weirdness"* reads an instrument nobody can calibrate, so the rerun gate
certifies a pass rather than forcing one; a three-role dispatch (generate / prune-only /
audit); the rerun trigger. · *Wire:* `SKILL.md` step 4 subagent dispatch, which already runs
lead and lens in isolation and is the nearest existing analogue. · *Absent:* no sampling
parameters are exposed anywhere in the repo, and no measure of weirdness exists — the same

**Flagged in passing** · The Builder named the prune-only role as the one mechanically

## CONSTRAINT REGION
*Mechanisms that force creativity by taking something away. The shared enemy: the model's
signature move, which is always available and therefore always taken.*

### C1 · The 19th Way ◆
*(owner idea 1)*

The persona produces 19 improvements to the plan, and each must **fail a stated
relatedness test** against all previous ones — different mechanism, different actor,
different failure mode, different timescale. Idea 12 cannot be idea 3 with a new noun.
The persona declares the axis on which each is unrelated. Ideas 1–6 will be mediocre;
that mediocrity is the toll paid to reach 19.

**Not native:** asked for 19 ideas, a model produces four real ones and fifteen
rephrasings. The non-adjacency test is what makes the 19 cost something.

**Status** · **ELIMINATED — MECHANISM ABSORBED** — UFO G13, 1–29. Scored **one point across
five axes**, all three panels placing it at or near the Irreducibility floor for the same
reason: it is its own prompt. Absorption **3–0**, the only unanimous absorption of the
tournament.

**Enhancements** · — (this entrant contributed rather than grew)

**What travelled** · The **four-axis non-adjacency test** — a new item must differ from every
prior one on a declared axis (mechanism, actor, failure mode, timescale), and the axis must
be named — into `M3`, THE ADJUDICATED LEDGER. The count of 19 and the
mediocrity-as-toll argument stay behind, as all three panels specified. The Skeptic called it
*"the rare absorption where the loser supplies the missing predicate of the winner's own
rule."*

**Gaps** · *None of its own.* Its irreducibility predicate is the finding: a base model handed
the instruction verbatim reproduces 70–95% of it. It needs no repo artifact, and that is
precisely why it lost. The gaps that matter now belong to `M3`.

### C2 · The Anti-Roster ◇

A persona defined entirely by refusal. `anti-roster/<slug>.md` contains no principles, no
methodology, no voice — only a prohibition list: two hundred phrases, moves, and framings
this persona may not use. The anti-monk cannot say *simplicity*, *enough*, *presence*,
*creation*, or *poverty*. Stripped of its vocabulary, the persona must find its position
again from somewhere else, and the somewhere-else is the product.

**Not native:** given a persona, a model reaches first for that persona's signature
vocabulary. Banning the vocabulary bans the shortcut.

**Status** · **WILDCARD BENCH** — Dog G6, 13–18, panels 2–1. Held because it took **Distance
+9 unanimous** and lost on mechanism, not on ambition. Revivable into any later round.

**Enhancements** · —

**Gaps** · *Build:* `anti-roster/<slug>.md` prohibition lists — two hundred phrases each,
across 24 personas; a checker that rejects on use of a banned phrase. · *Wire:* loaded
alongside the profile at `SKILL.md` step 2, and derivable in part from each profile's
Anti-Patterns and Voice sections (the same source `C6` compiles from). · *Absent:* no
`anti-roster/`; no compliance check — which is the Skeptic's decisive charge, that the
artifact is a prompt: *"200 banned phrases pasted into context is the whole mechanism."*
Building the checker is what would answer that charge.

### C3 · Idea Bankruptcy ◇

You may not deliver anything from your own first quartile. The persona generates N ideas;
the first N/4 are **destroyed unread** by the harness before any synthesis agent sees
them. Not deprioritized — deleted. The persona is told this in advance, which changes
what it generates from the first token.

**Not native:** models front-load their best-known answer. Guaranteed destruction of the
front-load makes producing the front-load pointless.

**Status** · **ELIMINATED** — Fence G4, 5–26, unanimous across all three panels. Absorptions
refused (one SUBSUMED, two ORTHOGONAL). Contributed nothing recorded.

**Enhancements** · —

**Gaps** · *Build:* a harness layer that destroys generated material before any synthesis
agent reads it. · *Absent:* there is **no harness layer between generation and synthesis** —
the same model does both, so "destroyed unread" is currently unenforceable by construction.

**Note** · The Ecologist named a defeat that infrastructure cannot fix: *"a persona told its
first quartile dies learns to front-load disposable ideas, so by use #10 the destruction
removes five deliberate throwaways and idea #6 is the same median answer the rule was built
to kill."* The mechanism trains its own defeat. Worth remembering before any future entrant
proposes announcing a control to the party it constrains.

### C4 · The Telegram Constraint ◇

Make expression cost something per unit. The persona delivers under a **priced medium**: a
1904 telegram at one dollar per word against a forty-dollar budget; a single index card; a
thirty-second voicemail; a woodblock with a fixed glyph count. The economics of the
medium, not a word-limit instruction, decide what survives — and what a persona sacrifices
under real cost reveals what it actually believes.

**Not native:** models treat "be concise" as a style note and compress uniformly. A priced
budget is an optimization problem with a different, revealing solution.

**Status** · **WILDCARD BENCH** — Moat G12. **Won the aggregate 18–11 and lost to a
commissioner overrule** [Ruling 8]; panels split 1–2 the other way. One of two bench entries
that won its game on points — see also `M7`, which won its game 19–7 unanimously and was
displaced by a revival rather than beaten.

**Enhancements** · —

**Gaps** · *Build:* a priced-medium renderer with real budget accounting (price per word
against a cap, not a word limit) and a **sacrifice log** recording what was cut — the log is
where the diagnostic lives. · *Wire:* an output-format option on `/temp-agency-plan` and
`/temp-agency-review`. · *Absent:* no output-length accounting anywhere in the repo, and
nothing captures what a persona dropped. The Skeptic's charge is that without the accounting,
*"strip the 1904 costume and A is `--max-words 40`"* — the budget is the whole difference and
it is the part that does not exist.

**Held because** · Its failure mode is the most valuable object in its packet: an all-hedges
telegram is a diagnostic on the **roster**, not on the pass.

### C5 · Notation Transposition ◇

Force the position through a notation that cannot hold prose — a recipe, a court docket, a
knitting pattern, a chess annotation, a liturgical rubric, a flight checklist, a circuit
diagram — and only then translate back. The notation's structural requirements make
commitments prose lets you skip: a recipe demands quantities and an order; a docket demands
parties, a motion, and a ruling; a checklist demands a challenge and a response.

**Not native:** models translate prose *into* diagrams, preserving the prose's evasions.
Authoring the notation *first* makes the gaps structural and visible.

**Status** · **ADVANCED → Sweet 16** — UFO G15, 19–16, unanimous on the winner and the
closest unanimous game of the round. All absorptions refused.

**Enhancements** · — (advances as written)

**Gaps** · *Build:* a notation library with the structural slots each form demands — a recipe
needs quantities and an order, a docket needs parties, a motion and a ruling, a checklist
needs a challenge and a response; a translate-back step; a check that the slots were actually
filled. · *Wire:* `SKILL.md` step 5 (synthesis), with the ordering constraint that the
notation is authored **before** the prose, not rendered from it afterward — reversing that
order is the entrant's own named failure. · *Absent:* no output-format machinery of any kind.
A notation whose slots are not enforced degrades to prose in costume, which is the failure the
entrant exists to prevent.

**Note** · The Skeptic placed it at the **Distance ceiling on every sentence it produces** —
the highest sustained Distance claim in the field.

### C6 · The Constraint Compiler ◆
*(owner idea 2, split: "text transformers")*

`bin/compile-constraints <slug>` reads a profile's Anti-Patterns and Voice sections and
emits a machine-checkable constraint set: banned n-grams, required section shapes, a
minimum count of domain-specific nouns, a forbidden-hedge list. Constraints are injected
as hard rules *and* verified post-hoc by a linter — compliance is checked, not trusted.

**Not native:** the profile currently *describes* its anti-patterns in prose the model may
or may not honor under pressure. A compiled, checked constraint is enforced.

**Status** · **WILDCARD BENCH + PARTIALLY ABSORBED** — Dog G5, 13–17, panels 2–1. Its checker
half travelled into `E5`; the entrant itself is held on the bench rather than discarded.

**Enhancements** · —

**What travelled** · The **era-lexicon checker pattern** — an external checker that rejects
any output containing a term from a compiled list — into `E5`, THE DATED SPECIALIST. The
**compile-from-Anti-Patterns half was explicitly refused**, because that half carries this
entrant's rival diagnosis: that voice prose, not concept availability, is the homogeneity
source. That diagnosis is why the entrant is on the bench and not eliminated.

**Gaps** · *Build:* `bin/compile-constraints <slug>` reading Anti-Patterns and Voice into
banned n-grams, required section shapes, a domain-specific-noun quota and a forbidden-hedge
list; a linter that verifies compliance post-hoc. · *Wire:* the Anti-Patterns and Voice
sections of `roster/*.md` are free prose today and would need a machine-readable shape — the
same structural-uniformity prerequisite `E4` needs for crossover. · *Absent:* no `bin/`, no
linter. The Skeptic's line is the honest summary of the gap: *"delete `bin/compile-constraints`
and nothing in the repo checks compliance at all."*

### C7 · Persona Grammar ◇

Stop trusting vibes; write a parser. Each persona gets a formal output grammar (BNF/PEG).
The investigative journalist's grammar requires every claim node to carry a `SOURCE` child
or be tagged `TIP`. The nuclear operator's requires every action node to carry `LIMIT`,
`INDICATION`, and `STOP` children. Output that fails to parse is rejected and regenerated.
The epistemology becomes syntactically enforced.

**Not native:** a model asked to cite sources cites some. A parser that rejects an
unparented claim node makes the citation structural rather than aspirational.

**Status** · **ELIMINATED — MECHANISM ABSORBED** — Fence G2, 8–23. Absorption ratified 2–1
[Ruling 3]; Builder and Ecologist independently returned ABSORBED with near-identical merged
sentences, having never seen each other's work.

**Enhancements** · —

**What travelled** · The **rejection primitive only** — output that fails the check is
rejected and regenerated without the model's consent — into `M5`, THE BINDING MAP. The
**per-persona grammar was explicitly refused**: both absorbing panels named it a second claim.

**Gaps** · *Build (were it revived on its own terms):* a BNF/PEG grammar per persona and a
parser in the dispatch loop. · *Absent:* no parser. But the decisive objection was structural
rather than infrastructural, and survives any amount of building — the Builder: *"a gate
certifies shape and cannot see content, and shape-enforcement narrows toward the median rather
than away from it."* A future entrant proposing syntactic enforcement should answer that first.

### C8 · Make the Problem Strange First ◇

Defamiliarize the **input**, not the output. Before any persona sees the task, a transform
strips its domain nouns and replaces them with variables: *users* → `ENTITY_A`,
*leaderboard* → `MECHANISM_B`, *cheating* → `BEHAVIOR_C`. Personas reason about the
abstracted structure, propose against it, and only afterward is the mapping restored.
Solutions that worked only because of a noun's connotations die in the abstraction.

**Not native:** the model's domain priors fire on the nouns before reasoning begins.
Removing the nouns removes the priors — you cannot pattern-match what you cannot name.

**Status** · **ADVANCED → Sweet 16** — Moat G10, 30–2, unanimous across all three panels. All
absorptions refused. Landed on the Distance ceiling reference near-verbatim.

**Enhancements** · Not an absorption — an **amendment** [Ruling 13 / Amendment 3], shared with
`A1` and `M1`. C8's scrimmage masked nouns by substitution inside a context that still held the
originals — its own defect test proved the untouched words (`open`, `List`, `distinct`) still
carried the priors the mask claims to remove (`s16-c8.md:110,121,88-94`). **Amended
definition:** the noun-masking transform runs in a context that emits only the abstracted
brief; the persona reasoning against it never shares a context with the raw brief, so no
untransformed word can leak a prior forward. A same-context run records the mask as NOT RUN.
This is additive to the carried defect below — blinding the context does not by itself fix the
verbs-and-adjectives overclaim.

**Gaps** · *Build:* a noun-masking transform with a stable mapping table and a restore step;
a decision on whether verbs and adjectives are in scope (see the carried defect). · *Wire:* a
pre-pass on the **brief**, before `SKILL.md` step 2 — the mirror of `A1`, which transforms the
**artifact**. Both need the same missing surface. · *Absent:* nothing in the repo transforms
the input before a persona reads it; the brief is passed through untouched.

**Carried defect** · The entrant overstates its own mechanism. *"Removing the nouns removes
the priors"* is false as stated, because verbs and adjectives carry priors too: *"Users **game**
the leaderboard to gain **unearned** status"* becomes *"ENTITY_A **games** MECHANISM_B to gain
**unearned** status"*, and the prior survives. It advanced 30–2 anyway; the stated mechanism is
simply broader than the real one, and the real one should be restated before it is built.

## APPARATUS REGION
*Mechanisms that make the repo executable — code, weights, tools, topology. The shared
enemy: markdown that asks a model to behave differently and hopes.*

### A1 · Lens Transformers ◆
*(owner idea 2, split: "Python libraries to more strictly adopt the lens")*

Make the lens a **program that runs on the artifact**, not a request to see it
differently. Each persona ships `transforms/<slug>.py`, a deterministic text transformer
applied to the artifact *before* the persona reads it. The investigative-journalist
transform strips every assertion of its supporting clause and returns a numbered list of
naked claims. The farmer transform re-sorts every line by how long it will still be true.
The monk transform deletes every sentence containing a superlative. The persona reads only
the transformed artifact.

**Not native:** "read this like a journalist" changes tone. Handing a model a document
where every claim has been mechanically severed from its support changes what it can see.

**Status** · **ADVANCED → Sweet 16** — Moat G9, 37–2. **The widest margin of the tournament**;
four of five axes at +9 unanimous and maximal. All absorptions refused.

**Enhancements** · Not an absorption — an **amendment** [Ruling 13 / Amendment 3], shared with
`C8` and `M1`: three independently-run scrimmages, each testing an unrelated mechanism,
converged on the identical gap without seeing each other's work. A1's scrimmage ran the
transform deterministically, but the responding pass still sat in the same context as the raw
artifact — the transform decorated the response instead of governing it (`s16-a1.md:117,127`).
**Amended definition:** each `transforms/<slug>.py` pass runs in a context that receives only
its transformed artifact; the persona then reasons and responds from inside that same
restricted context, never regaining sight of the raw original. A same-context run — transform
applied, but the responding pass can still see the untransformed artifact — records the
transform as NOT RUN, not merely weakened.

**Gaps** · *Build:* `transforms/<slug>.py` per persona — the journalist's claim-stripper, the
farmer's re-sort by how long each line stays true, the monk's superlative delete. · *Wire:*
applied to the artifact **before** the persona reads it (`SKILL.md` step 4);
`/temp-agency-review` is the natural first host, since it already takes an artifact as input.
· *Absent:* no `transforms/`, and no Python anywhere in the repo outside `docs/tournament/`.
Twenty-four transforms is twenty-four authored programs, which is the real cost — the same
24× authoring cost `A6`, `A2` and bench `C2` each carry.

**Note** · Shares its missing surface with `C8`: a pre-read transform layer. `A1` transforms
the artifact, `C8` transforms the brief. Building either builds most of the other.

### A2 · The Voice Oracle ◆
*(owner idea 3)*

Decouple voice from problem-solving so voice cannot be smoothed away. A tiny fine-tuned
model (Qwen3-0.6B/1.7B class) is trained on the persona's `knowledge/` pack plus synthetic
in-character dialogue, and its **only** job is to produce persona-voice reactions — never
solutions. The large model solves; the small model reacts in character; the large model
must respond to the reaction. The small model is too small to be diplomatic, and that is
the feature.

**Not native:** a large aligned model regresses toward its house voice under any pressure.
Separate weights cannot regress toward a house they do not have.

**Status** · **ADVANCED → Sweet 16** — UFO G14, 31–0. **A shutout** — the losing side scored
zero across five axes. Every panel placed it at the Irreducibility **ceiling**: the only
entrant in 32 that changes weights.

**Enhancements** · — (advances as written)

**Gaps** · *Build:* a per-persona training set (the `knowledge/` pack plus synthetic
in-character dialogue), a fine-tune of a Qwen3-0.6B/1.7B-class model, a local inference path,
and the react-then-respond loop that forces the large model to answer the small one. · *Wire:*
`SKILL.md` step 4. · *Absent:* the repo has no model artifacts, no inference dependency and no
training data — and the knowledge packs total **22,004 words across 18 specialists**, far too
small to train a voice on. Issue #21 is the prerequisite, and it needs no ML to start.

**Note** · Parked as a future state in issue #21. Highest irreducibility in the field and the
highest build cost in the field, by a wide margin on both.

### A3 · Persona Toolbelts ◆
*(owner idea 2, split: "scripts and shells")*

An expert is partly defined by the tools they reach for. Each persona owns a small set of
real CLI tools it **must** run and cite. The journalist gets `bin/claims`,
`bin/who-benefits`, and `git log --follow`. The farmer gets `bin/churn` (which files have
been touched most over two years) and `bin/seasons` (commit cadence by month). The
physicist gets `bin/units` and `bin/orders`. The output must include the invocation and
its raw result. No tool output, no pass.

**Not native:** models assert without measuring. A mandatory tool run replaces an
assertion with an observation.

**Status** · **ADVANCED → Sweet 16** — Fence G4, 26–5, unanimous across all three panels and
unanimous and maximal on Irreducibility. Advances **as written**, with a defect on the record.

**Enhancements** · — (advances as written)

**Gaps** · *Build:* the tools. `git log --follow` already exists; `bin/churn`, `bin/seasons`,
`bin/units` and `bin/orders` do not. Then either implement or drop the two costumed ones
below. · *Wire:* a tools list per persona — `roster/*.md` carries a Preferred Sources table
but no tools section — plus the "no tool output, no pass" gate that makes the run mandatory.
· *Absent:* no `bin/` directory at all. Shares that with `E1`, `E4` and bench `C6`; four
Sweet 16 and bench entrants are waiting on the same missing executable surface.

**Carried defect** · `bin/claims` and `bin/who-benefits` are not tools — *"a prompt wearing an
executable's name"* (Skeptic), *"an LLM judgment printed behind a shell prompt is method
exposure, not measurement"* (Builder), found independently by two panels. Four of six named
tools are real computations; two launder a model judgment into apparent measurement, which is
worse than stating the judgment plainly. The entrant's whole claim is that a mandatory tool run
replaces an assertion with an observation, and that holds only where the tool is a genuine
outside source of fact.

### A4 · The Committee of Strangers ◇

Two voices average; five to seven voices form factions. Replace the dyad with an N-body
deliberation carrying explicit political mechanics: a proposal needs a second, a defection
must state its price, and a minority report is preserved verbatim in the output. Positions
no single persona holds emerge from coalition-forming.

**Not native:** naive multi-agent prompting converges to consensus. Coalition and
defection rules make the disagreement itself the output format.

**Status** · **WILDCARD BENCH** — Moat G10, 2–30 **as written** (five to seven bodies with
coalitions, defection and minority reports). Held for a narrower **committee-of-3** variant.

**Enhancements** · —

**Gaps** · *Build:* an N-body dispatch — `SKILL.md` is hard-coded to a dyad at every step, so
the topology change is the work; a second/defect/minority-report protocol; a preserved-verbatim
minority slot in the output format. · *Absent:* no multi-agent structure beyond lead + lens,
and no detector for the Skeptic's named failure — *"a procedurally clean deliberation whose
minority report reads 'None; the committee reached consensus'"*, which launders the median
answer as a rigorously deliberated result.

**Held because** · On evidence this tournament generated: the three-panel structure judging
these games **is** a committee of strangers, and independent convergence between isolated
panels has been the single most reliable signal in the run. The 5–7 body version was blown out;

**Flagged for separate build** · *Priced defection* (Ecologist, G10): *"a price is a falsifiable
commitment a persona can be held to later, which is the only thing in this matchup that could

### A5 · The Hostile Environment ◇

Personas currently work in a laboratory. Put them in weather. The harness injects real
adverse conditions: a key fact is withheld from context; a stakeholder statement in the
brief is a deliberate lie; a hard token deadline cuts the pass short; a "budget cut"
removes the persona's best tool mid-task. A persona's response to degradation differs from
its response to comfort, and the difference reveals its actual priorities.

**Not native:** models reason *about* hypothetical constraints while retaining full
capability. A fact genuinely absent from context cannot be reasoned around.

**Status** · **ADVANCED → Sweet 16** — Dog G7, 27–13. **The only unanimous five-axis sweep of
the tournament**, three axes maximal. Absorption refused [Ruling 5].

**Enhancements** · — (advances as written; the commissioner declined the Ecologist's merge)

**Gaps** · *Build:* a context-degradation harness — withhold a named fact, plant a false
stakeholder statement, cut the pass at a token deadline, remove a persona's best tool
mid-task; plus a ground-truth key so the planted-lie variant can be scored after the fact.
· *Wire:* needs a context-assembly layer, which today amounts to "load both profile files"
in `SKILL.md` steps 2–3. · *Absent:* no context assembly, no token budgeting, no ground-truth
key. Shares the "remove something from the context window" surface with `C8` and `E2`.

**Note** · Implements the Irreducibility **high reference** almost literally — a fact genuinely
absent from context cannot be reasoned around — which is why all three panels swept it.

### A6 · The Understudy ◇

The most interesting version of an expert is the person who watched them and got it
slightly wrong. Every persona has an understudy generated by deliberate degradation: it
has the methodology but not the judgment, applies phase 3 where phase 1 belongs,
over-uses the signature move, and misidentifies which principle is load-bearing. The
understudy runs alongside, and its errors are treated as proposals — applying a framework
where it doesn't belong is precisely what the left-fielder does on purpose.

**Not native:** a model asked to "make a mistake" makes a labeled, safe, obviously-wrong
mistake. A systematically degraded copy makes mistakes that have internal logic.

**Status** · **ADVANCED → Sweet 16 (REVIVED)** — Fence G1: **won** the aggregate 12–8 and lost
the panel majority 2–1 to a commissioner overrule [Ruling 1], placed on the wildcard bench

Sweet 16 slot over both G8 entrants [Ruling 6]. The only entrant in the field to reach the
Sweet 16 by revival.

**Enhancements** · Not an absorption — an **amendment** [Ruling 16 / Amendment 6]. A6's
scrimmage produced all three degradation errors, but only via per-run manual prompting —
preserving the degradation depended on operator discipline, with no mechanism forcing it to
survive (`s16-a6.md:129`). **Amended definition:** before generation, a per-run degradation card
freezes the exact phase permutation, the one overused signature technique, and the false
load-bearing principle, each with one required output consequence; the card travels with both
outputs. Blocks silent normalization back to the parent persona's competent answer — the
required-consequence clause is what makes the degradation stick rather than drift.

**Gaps** · *Build:* a degradation generator — methodology without judgment: phase-order
scramble, signature-move over-use, misidentification of the load-bearing principle — and 24
understudy files. · *Wire:* runs alongside the real persona at `SKILL.md` step 4, with its
errors surfaced as **proposals** rather than corrected, which is a synthesis-format change at
step 5. · *Absent:* no `understudy/`. The Ecologist's cost objection stands and is a real
maintenance gap, not a rhetorical one: *"24 generated files that rot against the profiles they
were degraded from"* — with no regeneration path, the understudies drift out of sync the first
time a profile is edited.

**Note** · All three panels refused the merge with `E3` in G1, each saying the two defeat
**different reflexes** — authority inversion versus fidelity degradation. If the draw pairs
them again in the Sweet 16, that refusal is already on the record three times.

### A7 · Bracket as a Primitive ◆
*(owner idea 4, split: "workflows")*

The tournament is not a one-off; it is the repo's core operation. `/bracket <question>

Absorption is what distinguishes this from ranking: ranking discards, absorption
compounds, and the champion is a chimera that no round-one entrant resembles.

**Not native:** models rank, and ranking throws away everything below the top. A bracket
with absorption forces combination, and combination is where non-obvious ideas live.

**Status** · **ELIMINATED** — Dog G8, 7–19, unanimous across all three panels, all three
ORTHOGONAL. **The tournament eliminated its own mechanism**, judged by panels blind to what
they were evaluating.

**Enhancements** · —

**Gaps** · *Build:* `/bracket <question> --field 32` — a field generator, a seeded draw,
packet anonymization, three-panel dispatch, tally, and a box-score renderer. · *Absent:*
more than generalization. `tally.py` and `build_box_score.py` exist and do parse and render
real data, and `r32-draw-map.json` and `packets/` are real artifacts — but they were
hand-built for this one run, not produced by a generator or dispatcher. Repository-wide
inspection finds no field generator and no three-panel tournament dispatcher anywhere in the
repo; `SKILL.md`'s dispatch logic only ever selects and runs **one lead + one lens**, never a
multi-panel tournament. Those central capabilities are absent, not merely unparameterized —
what's already built is the back half of the pipeline (tally and render), not the front half
(generate and dispatch).

**Open finding against the format (unresolved)** · The Skeptic's charge: *"B is the entrant
that most flatters the process now evaluating it… 32 entrants with a refusal-by-default
absorption rule means 24 of them contribute nothing at all."* Measured at Round-of-32
completion (all four brackets, not the 12-of-16 interim count this figure previously carried

flagged-for-separate-build list (2) are three more. The charge is **not

### A8 · Scarcity Economy ◇

Personas speak for free, so they always speak. Give each a finite lifetime token budget
recorded in the repo. Being staffed costs; speaking costs by the word. A persona whose
contribution the user marks as noise pays a penalty; one marked decisive earns. Personas
**bid** to be staffed, and the bid is a one-line claim of what they will see that no one
else will. Bankrupt personas retire.

**Not native:** nothing in a prompt makes a model economize on relevance. A real ledger
with a real balance does.

**Status** · **WILDCARD BENCH** — UFO G16, 10–15, panels 2–1, CONTESTED on the split
[Ruling 10]. All absorptions refused.

**Enhancements** · —

**Gaps** · *Build:* a per-persona ledger with balances; a bid step (one line: what this persona
will see that no one else will); a staffing cost and a per-word charge; capture of the user's
noise/decisive verdict. · *Wire:* `SKILL.md` step 6 already asks the reflection question —
*"Did the [lens] angle add anything, or was it noise this time?"* — and **records nothing**.
That answer is the entrant's entire input signal, and it is currently discarded. · *Absent:*
no ledger, no persistence, no feedback storage — and no defense against the selection loop
below.

**Held because** · It was the only entrant in its matchup whose state differs at use #10.

**Its own judge's case against it** · The dissenting Ecologist wrote the sharpest argument
against its own pick, and it belongs on the record: *"'Contrarian — balance 0, RETIRED.' The
persona whose entire function is disagreement is the persona most often marked noise… personas
optimize for what gets marked decisive, which is a reinforcing loop toward the user's own
taste. A mechanism sold as 'economize on relevance' will in practice teach personas to economize
on **approval**, and an approval-selected roster converges on the median-of-this-user answer.
That is homogenization with a ledger attached."* Any revival must answer this first.

## MEMORY REGION
*Mechanisms that use history, measurement, and accountability. The shared enemy: a fresh
context window, which lets the model make the same excellent point forever.*

### M1 · The Homogeneity Auditor ◆
*(owner idea 4, split: "evals")*

Name the median answer out loud so it cannot be delivered. A dedicated adversarial
subagent receives the near-final output and produces exactly one artifact: *"Here is the
answer a competent model with no skill installed would have given."* If the auditor's
reconstruction overlaps the real output past a threshold, the overlapping sections are
rejected and regenerated. The auditor never proposes; it only accuses.

**Not native:** a model cannot see its own median from inside. A separate agent
explicitly generating the median makes it visible and therefore avoidable.

**Status** · **ADVANCED → Sweet 16 as THE BLIND AUDITOR** — Moat G12, lost the aggregate
11–18, won the panel majority 2–1, **commissioner overrule + entrant amended** [Ruling 8].

**Enhancements** · Not an absorption — an **amendment**, and the first of the tournament. The
auditor is **blinded**: it never sees the real output before reconstructing the median it will
be compared against. Sourced from the Ecologist, who named it *"the single edit that would most
improve this entry"*, and it answers the Builder's dissent directly — that the gate was *"applied
by the same model that wrote the text, reading text it has already seen."* · **Amended
definition:** *"A separate agent that has never seen the real output reconstructs the answer an
unskilled model would give; any section of the real output that overlaps it is rejected and
regenerated."* · This ruling set the standing rule for amendments: an entrant may be amended when
a panel identifies a specific improving edit, the edit serves the entrant's existing thesis, and
the amendment is recorded with its source.

**Second amendment** [Ruling 13 / Amendment 3], shared with `A1` and `C8`. M1's scrimmage ran
both the real pass and the blind-auditor pass inside one continuous Codex session, so "never
seen the real output" was never actually enforced and median independence could not be
established (`s16-m1.md:159-165,181`). **Amended definition (supersedes the blindness clause
above):** the auditor's reconstruction runs in a context separate from the one that produced the
real output, receiving the brief and nothing else; a same-context run — auditor and generator
sharing one session — records the auditor pass as NOT RUN, not merely unblinded.

**Gaps** · *Build:* a separate agent that receives the brief but not the output; an overlap
measure with a defensible threshold; a **section-level** reject-and-regenerate loop; and the
forced/chosen annotation below. · *Wire:* `SKILL.md` step 5, before synthesis is delivered.
· *Absent:* no overlap measure and no regeneration loop. The threshold is the whole mechanism
and is unspecified — the same missing instrument `M5` needs, and the same one that sank `M6`
and `E8`.

**Carried defect (unfixed by the amendment)** · The gate cannot distinguish *median because
unimaginative* from *median because correct*. It rejects overlap, and correct-and-obvious
answers overlap by definition, so it systematically punishes right answers for being reachable.
A fix is **proposed and commissioner-approved but untested**: have the blind auditor annotate
each claim in its reconstructed median as **forced** or **chosen**, and reject overlap only on
*chosen* claims. This is the sharpest open objection against any Sweet 16 entrant.

### M2 · The Novelty Gate ◆
*(owner idea 4, split: "CI")*

CI should be able to fail a pull request for being boring. A GitHub Action runs the repo's
evals and computes a novelty score — semantic distance from an unstaffed baseline plus
lexical distinctness from the persona's own prior outputs. Below threshold, the check
fails, with the too-familiar passages annotated inline. Creativity becomes a build status.

**Not native:** nothing currently punishes a smoothed output. A red check does.

**Status** · **ELIMINATED** — Fence G3, lost a **16–16 dead heat** to a commissioner tiebreak
[Ruling 2]. Took **Compounding +9 unanimous and maximal** — the strongest compounding score in
the field — against the field's most irreducible entrant. Contributed nothing recorded.

**Enhancements** · —

**Gaps** · *Build:* `.github/workflows/` — **the repo has no CI at all**; an embedding step; a
baseline unstaffed run to measure semantic distance from; a stored corpus of the persona's
prior outputs; inline PR annotation of the too-familiar passages. · *Absent:* no `.github/`,
no stored outputs, no baseline. Three prerequisites, none of which exist.

**Note for the Sweet 16** · Its elimination removed the field's **only CI entrant**. Nothing
still standing turns creativity into a build status, and several survivors (`M1`, `M5`, `E5`,
`E6`, `E2`) each specify a gate that has to run *somewhere*. Where those gates execute is now an
open question the bracket no longer has an entrant to answer.

### M3 · The Grudge Ledger ◇

A persona that repeats itself has stopped being a specialist and become a macro. Every
position a persona takes is appended to `ledger/<slug>.jsonl` as a canonical claim
statement. On future dispatch the persona is handed its own past positions with one rule:
**you may not restate any of these; you may extend, reverse, or refuse.** The persona must
grow or explicitly recant.

**Not native:** every session starts fresh, so the persona reaches for its signature move
forever. A persistent ledger makes the signature move unavailable after its first use.

> **⚠ Verified defect — the ledger does not exist.** All three blind panels independently
> checked the repo during Round-of-32 game 13 and found no `ledger/` directory, and —
> more damaging than its absence — *nothing named that would write it.* "Every position a
> persona takes is appended" is passive voice with no extractor, and a `.jsonl` file is
> data, not an executable. The Builder's verdict: *"Both are exhortation; A at least
> leaves a residue on disk."* This entrant won its bracket 29–1 and reached the Sweet 16
> **on a promise.** Anything built from it must supply the writer, the extractor that
> canonicalises a position into a claim string, and the gate that rejects a restatement —
> none of which currently exist.

**Status** · **ADVANCED → Sweet 16 as THE ADJUDICATED LEDGER** — UFO G13, 29–1, **absorption
ratified 3–0** [Ruling 9] — the only unanimous absorption of the tournament, with all three
panels independently selecting the same mechanism and passing it on all three tests without
seeing each other.

**Enhancements** · Absorbs The 19th Way's **four-axis non-adjacency test** and nothing else.
The ledger's rule was *"you may not restate any of these"* with no definition of "restate,"
which left it self-graded; the four axes supply exactly that definition and add no second claim.
The count of 19 and the mediocrity-as-toll argument stay behind. · **Merged definition:** *"A
persona is handed its own past claims and may not restate any of them, where 'restate' means
failing to differ in mechanism, actor, failure mode, or timescale."* · Builder: *"A rule that
appears to bind and does not is the worst state of the three."*

**Gaps** · *Build:* the four things all three panels named as missing — a **writer** (nothing
appends), an **extractor** that canonicalises a position into a claim string, the **`ledger/`
directory** itself, and the **gate** that rejects a restatement against the four axes. · *Wire:*
a post-pass at `SKILL.md` step 5 to write, and a pre-pass at step 3 to load the persona's own
history alongside `knowledge/`. · *Absent:* all four. This is the largest single gap among the
Sweet 16 relative to the entrant's stated claim — see the verified-defect note above, which is
why it reached this round **on a promise**.

### M4 · Failure Archaeology ◇

Keep the dead. Every rejected idea, losing bracket entrant, and abandoned plan is preserved
in `graveyard/` with a cause of death. It becomes mandatory reading before new work: a new
proposal must either differ from the graveyard or **explicitly resurrect** — stating what
changed to make the dead idea live. The repo accumulates a negative space as informative as
its positive one.

**Not native:** models do not remember what did not work. A graveyard turns the repo's own
failures into a constraint on its future.

**Status** · **ELIMINATED — MECHANISM ABSORBED** — Moat G11, 14–17. Won **Irreducibility +9
unanimous** and **Compounding +5 unanimous** — its two strongest axes, both maximal or near —
and still lost. Absorption ratified 2–1 [Ruling 7].

**Enhancements** · —

**What travelled** · **Killed hybrids with a recorded cause of death** into `E4`, THE BREEDING
PROGRAM — narrowly scoped, explicitly not this entrant's full claim over every rejected idea in
the repo.

**Why it lost, worth keeping** · All three panels reached the same structural conclusion
independently, and the Skeptic reached it **against its own bias axis**: *"A is a filter on the
existing generative process that reliably moves you from #12 to #13 and structurally never
further, while B is a **different generative process**, which is what the frame says reaching
#99 requires; A wins my own bias axis and I still vote against it on this ground."*

**Gaps** · *Build:* `graveyard/` with a cause-of-death record per entry, a mandatory pre-work
read, and a resurrection protocol (state what changed to make the dead idea live). · *Absent:*
no `graveyard/` — though `docs/tournament/` **is already a graveyard for sixteen dead ideas**,
unstructured and consulted by nothing. That is the cheapest available first instance of this
mechanism, and it exists today.

### M5 · The Idea-Space Map ◆
*(owner idea 4, split: "evals" — sharpened to coverage rather than score)*

Measure where the repo has **never** gone. Embed every output the repo has ever produced,
cluster them, and render a coverage map with the *empty regions labeled*. The deliverable
is not a score; it is a list of directions this repo has never once pointed. Future
dispatches are steered toward the voids. Exploration becomes navigable instead of hopeful.

**Not native:** a model has no view of its own output distribution. An external map of
what is missing is information it cannot generate from inside.

**Status** · **ADVANCED → Sweet 16 as THE BINDING MAP** — Fence G2, 23–8, **absorption ratified
2–1** [Ruling 3]. Builder and Ecologist independently returned ABSORBED with near-identical
reasoning and near-identical merged sentences, having never seen each other's work.

**Enhancements** · Absorbs Persona Grammar's **rejection primitive only** — output that fails
the check is rejected and regenerated without the model's consent. The per-persona grammar is
**explicitly refused**; both absorbing panels named it a second claim. · **Merged definition:**
*"Cluster every output the repo has ever produced and reject any new output that lands in a
region the corpus has already filled."* · The map was the thesis; the gate is what makes it
binding rather than advisory. · **Dissent preserved (Skeptic):** the Map's own text disclaims
enforcement, and a rejection threshold imports novelty-over-correctness — *"punishing the right
answer for being the answer already given… a map that forbids is a fence, and a fence refuses
the small repair in front of you on the grounds that someone already repaired one like it."*

**Gaps** · *Build:* an output corpus — nothing is stored today; `docs/receipts/` holds four
hand-written prose receipts, not machine-readable outputs; an embedding and clustering step; a
renderer that labels the **empty** regions; and the reject-and-regenerate gate. · *Wire:* a
write at `SKILL.md` step 5 (to grow the corpus) and a read at step 1 (to steer toward voids).
· *Absent:* the corpus is the hard prerequisite. The map has nothing to cluster until dispatches
are recorded, and no dispatch has ever been recorded.

**Note for the Sweet 16** · `M5` and `M1` gate on *different* quantities — `M5` rejects any
output landing in a region the historical corpus has already occupied; `M1` rejects overlap
with a separately reconstructed unskilled-median answer — but both carry the same unresolved
Skeptic-style objection: neither can tell a correct answer from an unimaginative one. If the
draw pairs them, that shared objection is the game, not a shared gate.

### M6 · The Ratchet ◇

Monotonic creativity: the floor only rises. Every accepted output sets a novelty floor, and
nothing may be accepted afterward that scores below it on the same axis. The bar moves up
permanently and never down. When the ratchet locks — when nothing can clear the floor —
that is the signal to change the mechanism, not to lower the bar.

**Not native:** quality bars drift downward under deadline pressure, invisibly. A ratchet
that cannot descend converts silent drift into an explicit, dated crisis.

**Status** · **ELIMINATED** — Dog G7, 13–27, losing the only unanimous five-axis sweep of the
tournament. Its own absorption into the winner was proposed by the Ecologist and **refused by
the commissioner** [Ruling 5]. Contributed nothing recorded.

**Enhancements** · —

**Gaps** · *Build:* the novelty scorer it presumes — the same instrument `M1` and `M5` still
owe and that `E8` was sunk by; a per-axis floor record; a lock-detection signal that fires when
nothing can clear the floor. · *Absent:* the scorer. Both of this entrant's ceiling-level claims
rest on it, and the Ecologist's verdict is the clean statement of the gap: *"it ships the ratchet
without the pawl."*

**Note** · The Builder anticipated and rejected the merge the Ecologist proposed: *"that absorbs
A's silhouette, not A's mechanism: it ratchets an input condition where A ratchets an output
score."* Worth keeping as the model of a well-drawn absorption refusal.

### M7 · The Adversarial Collaboration Contract ◇

Make disagreement falsifiable and dated. Before the work ships, lead and lens co-sign a
written prediction: *"If the lens is right, X will be observable by date D; if the lead is
right, Y."* It is recorded in `bets/` with a resolution date, and the repo periodically
resolves them. Personas accumulate track records, and one that is consistently wrong loses
standing.

**Not native:** model disagreements evaporate the moment the session ends. A dated,
resolvable bet makes them accountable — and, more importantly, forces the disagreement to
be about something *observable*.

**Status** · **WILDCARD BENCH** — Dog G8. **Won 19–7, unanimous across all three panels**, and
did not advance: the commissioner revived The Understudy into this Sweet 16 slot [Ruling 6].
The only bench entry that was never beaten — *displaced by a revive, not defeated.*

**Enhancements** · —

**Gaps** · *Build:* `bets/` holding a dated, resolvable prediction co-signed per dispatch; a
resolution step that runs **on a date rather than in a session**; a per-persona track record
that actually changes who gets staffed. · *Wire:* co-signed at `SKILL.md` step 5, read back at
step 1 (lead selection). · *Absent:* no `bets/`, no scheduler, no standing per-persona record.

**Note** · Its decisive praise names the exact thing this repo lacks everywhere, not just here —
the Ecologist: *"A is the only mechanism in this packet with a feedback path from outcome back
to structure — a resolved bet changes who gets staffed next time."* Nothing in the repo today
has a feedback path from outcome back to structure.

### M8 · The Reverse Brief ◇

Derive the question the answer is actually answering. After producing a recommendation, the
persona writes the problem statement for which that recommendation would be the *perfect*
answer, then diffs it against the real problem. The gap is a first-class output. Very
often, the recommendation turns out to be a perfect answer to a problem nobody has.

**Not native:** models validate answers against the question. This inverts the arrow and
exposes the reframing that was smuggled in during the answering.

**Status** · **ELIMINATED** — UFO G14, 0–31. A shutout: zero points across five axes, the
lowest score in the field. Contributed nothing recorded.

**Enhancements** · —

**Gaps** · *None.* It requires no repo artifact, and that is exactly why it lost — the Skeptic:
*"a paragraph any competent prompt already produces,"* whose characteristic failure *"silently
returns the default answer with a clean bill of health."*

**Note** · Read alongside `C1` (1 point) and `E7` (2 points), this is the field's clearest
signal about what the ruler rewards: an entrant that is fully contained in its own prompt scores
at the Irreducibility floor no matter how good the idea reads. Useful as a filter when the next
field is generated.
