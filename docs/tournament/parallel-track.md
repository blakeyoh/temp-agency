## Separate-build mechanisms surfaced by the panels

### Disagreement sourced from differing evidence

Two isolated readers receive different transformed evidence. The disagreement is caused by
the information boundary, not by an instruction to perform conflict. Origin: G9 Ecologist.
Also recorded as `CR-R32-G9` in `collision-residue.md`.

### Priced defection

A defection states the falsifiable evidence, concession, resource or observation that would
buy support. Origin: G10 Ecologist. Also recorded as `CR-R32-G10`.

### Prune-only role

The COLD role in Temperature Choreography may remove members of a generated set and is
mechanically forbidden from adding anything. Subset membership makes the asymmetry verifiable
without a weirdness instrument. Origin: G15 Builder.

## Elite 8 coalition record

The Elite 8 contact runs will add relationships here using this vocabulary:

| Relationship | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AMPLIFYING** | The contact output travels farther while preserving both mechanisms' integrity |
| **DAMPING** | One mechanism pulls the other toward its default failure |
| **PARASITIC** | One appears to benefit only by consuming the other's output without contributing a distinct operation |
| **ORTHOGONAL** | Both remain useful and the contact run adds nothing |

Coalitions never change entrant definitions and never count as absorption.
