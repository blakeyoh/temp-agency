# Community Harvest Food Bank of Northeast Indiana (CHFB) — context

Regional hunger-relief food bank, Fort Wayne, IN. Founded 1983 after the International
Harvester plant closure. 501(c)(3). HQ: 999 E Tillman Rd. Serves 9 counties (Adams, Allen,
DeKalb, Huntington, LaGrange, Noble, Steuben, Wells, Whitley). Feeding America affiliate;
member of Feeding Indiana's Hungry (FIsH).

## Scale (FYE June 2025)
- Revenue $26,989,668 (+10% YoY); expenses $27,053,582; net assets $16.58M; cash $3.17M
- 10.9M–17.8M lbs food/yr; 9,917,005 meal equivalents; 91,500–112,100 unique clients
- 51–59 FTE staff. Volunteers provide >50% of functional labor hours.
- 300+ member agencies; 250+ referral/voucher partners
- Marilyn and Joel Salon Endowment (target $5M) funds fleet, cold chain, tech

## Programs
Farm Wagon (mobile drive-through pantry, rural), Community Cupboard (client-choice grocery
pantry, voucher referral, weight-based allowances), Saturday Helping Hands, CSFP + SeniorPak
(seniors, USDA), Kids BackPack + TeenPak (youth weekend meals), Harry & Jeanette Weinberg
Produce Preservation Center (first US food bank with on-site commercial blanch/chill/blast-freeze line,
opened Aug 2015), wholesale distribution to 300+ agencies.

## Known strengths
Cold-chain/processing capability; 9-county last-mile fleet; CPA-trained CEO; clean Single
Audits; GuideStar Platinum; strong board (industrial automation, banking, CPA firm, trucking).

## Known weaknesses
>50% volunteer labor limits tech complexity; 300+ member agencies run wildly divergent systems
(Macola ERP at HQ vs. spreadsheets and paper registries at agencies); lean <60 FTE staff with no
slack for pilots; executive culture is risk-averse on operating expenditure and requires proven
cost recovery.

## Opportunities
"Food is Medicine" / Parkview Health SDOH integration; route optimization + client SMS for rural
counties with no public transit; capacity-building grants (Feeding America) and the Salon Endowment
as non-operating capital; Partner Advisory Council to raise agency standards.

## Threats
Food inflation raising client demand; fuel, maintenance, refrigeration utility costs; USDA
commodity volatility (TEFAP/CSFP, farm bill delays); rural broadband/cellular dead zones breaking
cloud-dependent mobile tools.

## Culture note
Accounting-led executive culture. Abstract impact narratives fail; auditable unit economics win.
Anything requiring multi-day training fails against a rotating volunteer floor.
