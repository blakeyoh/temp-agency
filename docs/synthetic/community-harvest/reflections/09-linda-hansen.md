# Linda Hansen — Reflection

I come to this questionnaire the way I come to a Tuesday morning route sheet — already running numbers in my head before anyone hands me the paper. Cold this week, first frost talk out in LaGrange, and I'm thinking about whether the Kurtz load holds in the truck an extra hour if we're delayed at the second site. That's the mindset. Not skeptical of the exercise, just not able to set the actual work down to answer it.

What did I want out of this career? I wanted to be useful in a way you could see — a truck showing up when it said it would, at a site people could count on, with food that wasn't half-spoiled by the time it got there. I got that. Nine counties, drive-through, no questions asked at the tailgate. I'm proud of that model — we took a walk-up line and built something that meets people where the roads actually go. I'm proud that I know my sites by name, and I notice, hard, when someone stops coming. That's not a metric anybody upstairs tracks, but it's the truest signal I've got about whether we're actually still serving.

What wears me down is the gap between the agricultural calendar and the org's calendar. Gluts don't wait for budget cycles. Frost doesn't care about the fiscal year. I spend a lot of energy translating what the season is telling me into language that gets heard three floors up, and half the time it doesn't land until it's already too late to act on it.
