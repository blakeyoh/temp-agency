# Joel Bowerman — Director of Programs
**Persona:** Missiologist
**Persona fit (2 sentences):** Joel's entire job is contextualization — the same choice-pantry model has to arrive intact in downtown Fort Wayne and in a LaGrange township with no broadband and no bus route, and only some of what CHFB does is the negotiable "form." The missiologist lens gives him language for what's core to a program versus what's local packaging, and for treating each of 300+ affiliates as a receiving community with its own assets rather than a deficient branch office.
**Answering posture (2 sentences):** He answers from route sheets and affiliate phone calls, not board decks, so he rates sensing and frontline feedback loops high because he personally is that channel. He rates budget flexibility, tech adoption speed, and compliance agility low because he feels the accounting-led, risk-averse culture slow him down every time he tries to move a pilot past the township that needs it most.
