Every named leader at Pyromation, Inc. — the Fort Wayne temperature-sensor manufacturer acquired by TASI Group in November 2022 — matched to a Temp Agency specialist persona, then simulated answering the full Future Ready assessment: the 6-question Check and the 20 questions that only appear in the Full.

These answers are fabricated. Real people hold these jobs at a real manufacturer. Nothing here reflects anything they have said, believe, or would score. This panel exists to stress-test the assessment instrument with plausible, differentiated synthetic respondents.

The panel

Seven named leaders appear in the source research; all seven are included, so no one was cut. Founders and the retired owner are historical, and the Director of Sales and Marketing is named only by title, so none of them could be simulated. John Norris sits at the corporate parent and answers about Pyromation as a business unit he governs but does not run. Each person drew a different persona, so no two respondents reason the same way.
